Paper in.
Attachment out.

Scan 2 PDF puts a Scan button inside Outlook. Scan one page or a whole stack from the feeder, and it lands in the email you're writing as one tidy PDF.

Get the add-in Download the bridge for Windows

Free and open source. Works in new Outlook, classic Outlook and Outlook on the web. Scanning needs Windows 10 or 11.

Now in Microsoft's review for the Outlook add-ins store and the Microsoft Store.

Set it up once. After that it's one click.

  1. Install the bridge

    A small Windows app that lets Outlook reach your scanner. Double-click it and choose Yes. It starts by itself every time you sign in.

  2. Add Scan 2 PDF to Outlook

    Add it from the manifest file today. Once Microsoft approves it, it'll be in Outlook's add-ins store too.

  3. Scan from any new email

    Click Scan to PDF on the ribbon, scan, check the pages, then Attach.

What it handles

Flatbed, feeder, both sides
One page from the glass or a full stack from the document feeder, including double-sided.
Check before you send
Open any page full size, rotate it, move it, or remove it. Scan more pages into the same file.
One PDF or separate JPEGs
Send everything as a single PDF, or one JPEG per page with your rotations applied.
Sized for email
Pick 150, 200 or 300 dpi, color, grayscale or black and white, and image quality. Letter, Legal or A4.
Works with your scanner
Any scanner with a Windows driver. If the Windows Scan app sees it, Scan 2 PDF does too.
No scanner nearby?
Add JPG or PNG photos and turn them into a PDF. On a PC with no scanner, try the built-in demo scanner.

Your documents never leave your desk.

The bridge runs on your own PC and only answers the Scan 2 PDF add-in. Pages go from your scanner into your email draft and nowhere else. There's no account, no upload and no tracking.

Questions

Which scanners work?

Any scanner or multifunction printer with a Windows (WIA) driver, which covers most office scanners. If it works with the Windows Scan app, it works here.

Why does Windows say "Windows protected your PC"?

Windows shows that for newly published apps it hasn't seen many times yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The Microsoft Store version, once approved, installs without this message.

Does it work on a Mac or in a browser?

The add-in runs in Outlook on Mac and on the web, but scanning needs the bridge, which is Windows-only. Elsewhere you can still use Add images from file to turn photos into a PDF attachment.

Where do my scans go?

From your scanner to the add-in on the same PC, then into your email as an attachment. Nothing is sent to Aghapy Tech Services or anyone else.

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, subscription or trial. The source code is on GitHub under the MIT License.

The add-in says it can't reach the scanner service

Make sure the bridge icon is in your system tray. If it isn't, open Scan2PdfBridge.exe. More fixes are on the help page.
