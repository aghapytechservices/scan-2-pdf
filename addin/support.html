Scan 2 PDF help

Set up the Scan 2 PDF add-in for Outlook and the bridge app for Windows, fix common scanning problems, and get in touch.

Set up

To scan from a scanner you need two parts: the add-in in Outlook, and the bridge on the Windows PC your scanner is connected to. To scan with your phone, you only need the add-in.

  1. Install the bridge

    Get it free from the Microsoft Store and open it once from the Start menu. It starts by itself every time you sign in, and the Store keeps it updated. No Store on your PC? Use the standalone download.

  2. Add the add-in to Outlook

    Download the manifest, open aka.ms/olksideload, then choose My add-ins, Add a custom add-in, Add from file.

  3. Scan from a new email

    Start a new email or reply and click Scan to PDF. Pick your scanner, scan, check the pages, then Attach.

    No scanner nearby? Choose Scan with phone, point your phone's camera at the QR code, and photograph each page. There's no app to install, and the pages appear in the panel as you send them.

The Outlook add-in is in Microsoft's review. Once approved, you'll also find it in Outlook's add-ins store.

Fix a problem

The bridge's status page, 127.0.0.1:52801, shows its version, your scanners and its settings. Open it on the PC with the scanner.

The panel says "Scanner service not reachable"
  • Look for the Scan 2 PDF icon in the system tray (click the ^ next to the clock). If it isn't there, open Scan2PdfBridge.exe.
  • If Outlook or your browser asks to let the add-in connect to apps on this device, choose Allow.
  • Open 127.0.0.1:52801 on the same PC. If the status page doesn't load, the bridge isn't running.
  • Then click Retry in the panel.
The bridge doesn't start after I restart the PC
  • Right-click the tray icon and make sure Start with Windows is checked.
  • Using version 1.1 or older? Download the latest version and double-click it. It replaces the old one and fixes start-up.
  • In Settings, Apps, Startup, check that Scan 2 PDF Bridge isn't turned off.
  • Still using the standalone download? Switching to the Microsoft Store version gives you automatic updates. Exit the standalone one from its tray icon first.
I can't find the Scan to PDF button in Outlook
  • The button appears only while you're writing an email: a new message, reply or forward.
  • In new Outlook and Outlook on the web, it may be under the Apps button on the message toolbar.
  • Still missing? Check that Scan 2 PDF is listed under My add-ins at aka.ms/olksideload.
No scanner is listed

The bridge uses Windows' built-in scanner support (WIA). Make sure the scanner is on and shows up in the Windows Scan app, and install the manufacturer's driver if it doesn't. Then click Retry. If you see "Demo scanner (sample pages)", Windows didn't find a real scanner.

A scan fails with a scanner error

Try a different resolution or paper size, and check for a paper jam or an open cover. If it keeps failing, right-click the tray icon, choose Open log folder, and send us bridge.log.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

That message can appear for the standalone download. The Microsoft Store version installs without it. For the standalone download, choose More info, then Run anyway. You'll only see it the first time.

The attachment is too large

Use 150 dpi, grayscale, or a lower JPEG quality under Advanced in the panel. The panel warns you before attaching anything over 20 MB.

Can I use it on a Mac?

Yes. The add-in works in Outlook for Mac, but the scanner connection is Windows-only for now. Choose Scan with phone to photograph pages with your phone, or scan with Image Capture or Preview (File, Import from Scanner), save as PDF or JPEG, and click Add PDFs or images from file or drag the files onto the panel.

Does it work on iPhone, iPad or Android?

Your phone works as the camera. While writing an email in Outlook on your computer, choose Scan with phone, scan the QR code with your phone and photograph each page. Pages arrive in the panel straightened and cleaned up. Outlook on phones and tablets doesn't run add-ins while you're writing an email, so the email itself is written on a computer.

The phone page says the code has expired

Each QR code works for 15 minutes. In Outlook, choose Show a new code in the phone box, or close it and choose Scan with phone again, then scan the new code. Pages already received stay in the panel.

Phone photos come out crooked or cropped wrong

Put the page on a darker, plain surface with even light, and fit the whole page in the photo. If the edges are still off, tap Adjust edges on the phone and drag the four corners onto the page's corners. Color keeps stamps and highlights; Original skips the cleanup.

Can I combine existing PDFs into one attachment?

Yes. Add PDFs, JPGs or PNGs with Add PDFs or images from file or by dragging them onto the panel. Reorder, rotate or remove pages, then attach them as one PDF. PDF pages keep their original quality.

Advanced settings

For IT and self-hosting. Most people never need these.

Allow another add-in address or change the port

Create appsettings.user.json in the bridge's data folder (the status page shows the exact path), restart the bridge, and add:

{
  "ScanBridge": {
    "ExtraAllowedOrigins": [ "https://scan.yourcompany.com" ]
  }
}
Install silently or remove the bridge (standalone version)

For the standalone download, run Scan2PdfBridge.exe --install --quiet to install for the signed-in user without prompts, or --uninstall to remove it. The download also includes install.cmd and uninstall.cmd, which you can double-click, plus PowerShell versions. If PowerShell says running scripts is disabled, use the .cmd files or run powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\install.ps1.
