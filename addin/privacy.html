Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Scan 2 PDF ("the add-in") is published by Aghapy Tech Services. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Scan 2 PDF Outlook add-in, the Scan 2 PDF Bridge app for Windows, and this website.

Summary

Your scanned documents never leave your computer except as attachments to the email you are writing. Aghapy Tech Services does not receive, store, or have access to your scans, your emails, or your Microsoft 365 account.

What the add-in does with your data

  • Scanned pages. The Scan 2 PDF Bridge app runs on your own PC and reads images from your scanner. It sends them only to the add-in running on the same PC (over the local loopback address 127.0.0.1). The add-in builds the PDF or JPEG files in your browser and adds them to your email through Outlook. No scan is uploaded to any server operated by Aghapy Tech Services.
  • Your email. The add-in only adds attachments to the message you are composing. It does not read your message contents, recipients, mailbox, or contacts.
  • Settings. Your scan preferences (scanner, resolution, color, paper size, output format) are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to us.
  • Bridge log. The bridge keeps a small log file on your PC (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Scan2PdfBridge\logs) with technical events such as "scan started" and error messages, to help with troubleshooting. It contains no images and is never sent anywhere automatically.

This website and hosting

The add-in's web files are hosted on Vercel. Like most web hosts, Vercel may process standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and request time) in server logs to deliver and secure the service. We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking on this site or in the add-in.

Microsoft

Outlook and Microsoft 365 are provided by Microsoft. Your use of them is covered by Microsoft's own privacy statement. Attachments you add are handled by Outlook and your mail service like any other attachment.

Children

The add-in is a business productivity tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and publish the new version on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact Aghapy Tech Services through aghapyts.com or open an issue on GitHub.
